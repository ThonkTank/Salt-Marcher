# Direct GM quality work

The existing GM-QA role now performs the complete improvement cycle itself: use real GM workflows in Dungeon Editor, suitable available competing products and the simplest usable alternative; identify useful differences; implement improvements; then retest the actual UI and appropriate existing product tests. Known findings and previous scenarios do not limit new questions. Keep observed results, inferences and untested effects distinct.

Retire the former study and delegation process from active work. There are no required tester/reviewer/implementation chains, independent retests, waves, preregistration, blindness, rating quotas, sealed packages or full methodology test suites. Historical protocols, scenarios, scripts and evidence remain intact at their original paths, including technical imports required by the existing UI adapter. They are historical material rather than another optional QA workflow.

A short own checkpoint records tested versions, useful findings, changes, evidence references, real blockers and the next step. The exact previous ledger remains available as history. Existing completed work is reused, never restarted to satisfy the retired process. Archive genuinely completed child chats after preserving their return; preserve main roles, open approvals, project assignments and original data.

Reuse the existing isolated native UI helper and protected resource launcher. Recorder begin/resume state is technical adapter state, not study registration. Preserve actual save/close and process-exit checks. Repair infrastructure only for a concretely blocked needed capability. Missing access or a resource deferral does not prevent other useful work. Development continues without a QA freeze or additional review gate.

The four main roles retain their independent mandates and do not exchange messages or read each other's chat histories or control judgments. Planner and Realist may independently inspect product evidence and the direct workflow. Repository sync at the start and end, resource limits, protection of others' work and existing publication restrictions remain in effect. Automation identities, schedules and permissions are unchanged.

Transition validation: the shared helper passes shell syntax validation and its three existing configuration/exit/EOF tests. Existing adapter imports load without starting a GUI job. The launcher, runtime backend, protocol, schemas, scenarios, historical tests and protected frozen evidence are unchanged. Private content and raw personal UI evidence remain local. This process change does not claim a new product acceptance or completed competitor comparison.
